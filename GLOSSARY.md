# Kazi's website

A single-screen interactive piece after Takenobu Igarashi's _Design News_ 113 cover: the word KAZI built from upright colored sheets you can rustle.

## Language

**Panel**:
One thin upright sheet; a vertical slice of a letter. Where the slice crosses a counter it has gaps, but it still moves and Chimes as one.
_Avoid_: Page, card, slice, sheet

**Spine**:
The fixed vertical edge of a Panel, on the face where the word's depth starts; Panels bend away from it like pages from a book's spine.
_Avoid_: Hinge, base, bottom

**Stack**:
The row of Panels that together form one letter.
_Avoid_: Letter mesh, glyph, block

**Rustle**:
The pointer-driven bend of Panels and their spring back to rest, like thumbing through records in a crate.
_Avoid_: Flip, wobble, deform

**Sway**:
The gentle ambient motion of Panels when no pointer drives them.
_Avoid_: Wind, idle animation

**Chime**:
The note a Panel plays when Rustled past; its pitch comes from the Panel's length, like a real chime.
_Avoid_: Flick, click, tick, note

**Sound toggle**:
The corner button, drawn as a tiny Stack, that turns Chimes on or off.
_Avoid_: Mute button, speaker icon

**Loading mark**:
The tiny Stack that rises in place while the piece loads on a slow connection, before the Panels take over.
_Avoid_: Spinner, loader, preloader

**Byline**:
The small corner line saying where Kazi works.
_Avoid_: Footer, caption, tagline

**Grain**:
The subtle ink speckle over the whole piece that makes it read as a risograph print; it re-rolls in small steps, like a hand-made print, and holds still for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
_Avoid_: Noise, texture, riso filter
